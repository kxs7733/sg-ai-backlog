The game moves up a step after 4 of the last 5 sums are right first try, and back down if they are mostly missed. You can also set the step here. Ticked games appear as buttons on the home screen.

Choosing a voice plays a sample. For a nicer one, download a Premium or Enhanced English voice in the iPad's Settings (Accessibility, Spoken Content or Read & Speak, Voices); it should then appear in this list.
